For instance, if a single job is defined and deleted 10 days later, then that job is billed as $0.1/31 times 10 which is $0.03.  Free usage  Each Google billing account gets 3 jobs per month free. Note that the free tier is measured at the account level not the project level. For example, if your account has 5 projects with 2 jobs each, you will have 3 free jobs and 7 paid jobs.
