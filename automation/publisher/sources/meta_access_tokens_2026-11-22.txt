…ort-lived and long-lived tokens {#termtokens} Access tokens come in two forms: short-lived and long-lived. Short-lived tokens typically last about one to two hours, while long-lived tokens last about 60 days. Do not depend on these lifetimes remaining the same — they may change without warning or expire early. See [handling errors](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/facebook-login/access-tokens/debugging-and-error-handling) for more information. Access tokens generated via web login are short-lived, but you can [convert them to long-lived tokens](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/facebook-login/guides/access-tokens/get-long-lived) by making a server-side API call with your app secret.
